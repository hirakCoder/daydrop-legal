2. Countdown Star — Best track record

Countdown Star by Joseph Merrill has the largest rating count of any countdown app we found: 214,877 ratings at 4.8★. It has been around for over a decade and covers the essentials well.

  • 4.8★ from 214,877 ratings — the most social proof on this list
  • 17 languages, Apple Watch support per its listing
  • A separate ad-free version for $1.99, one-time — genuinely cheap
  • Its listing doesn't mention Dynamic Island, Live Activities, or AI backgrounds
Verdict: The safe, proven choice. If you want a countdown app that hundreds of thousands of people already rated highly and a $1.99 one-time price, this is it.

3. Countdown by Find Appiness — Most languages

Countdown by Find Appiness LLC is another heavyweight: 4.8★ from 183,471 ratings, 28 supported languages (the most here), and per its listing it covers Lock Screen widgets, StandBy, and Apple Watch.

  • 4.8★ from 183,471 ratings
  • 28 languages — best-in-class localization
  • Lock Screen, StandBy, and Apple Watch per its listing
  • Free download with in-app purchases; the free tier includes ads
  • Its listing doesn't mention Dynamic Island, Live Activities, or AI backgrounds
Verdict: Best if you need a non-English interface beyond the common languages, or want a hugely proven app and don't mind ads on the free tier.

4. TimeCount — Widget-first design

TimeCount by App Forgeron is built around its widgets — the countdown on your Home Screen is the product. 4.6★ from 23,432 ratings.

  • Clean, widget-centric design
  • 4.6★ from 23,432 ratings
  • 6 languages
  • Its listing doesn't mention Dynamic Island, Live Activities, Apple Watch, or AI backgrounds
Verdict: A solid middle option if widgets are all you want and you like its look.

5. Big Days — Simple and free

Big Days by Vittorio Sorbera keeps it minimal: events, dates, countdowns. 4.6★ from a small base of 590 ratings, 12 languages, and StandBy support per its listing.

  • Simple, focused interface
  • 12 languages, StandBy per its listing
  • Small rating base (590 ratings)
  • Its listing doesn't mention Dynamic Island, Live Activities, or AI backgrounds
Verdict: Fine for basic countdowns if minimalism appeals; less proven than the two heavyweights above.

Which countdown app should you choose?

If you want AI backgrounds, Dynamic Island, and the deepest iOS integration: DayDrop. It's the only app on this list whose listing includes those features.

If you want the most proven app at the lowest price: Countdown Star — 214,877 ratings and a $1.99 one-time ad-free version.

If you need the widest language support: Countdown by Find Appiness, with 28 languages.

If you just want a free, simple widget: TimeCount or Big Days — or DayDrop's free tier, which includes unlimited countdowns without ads.
