1. Countdown by Find Appiness — most proven, most languages

The biggest dedicated countdown app on the US store by ratings: 4.81★ from 187,887. Creating events and reminders is free; Home Screen, Lock Screen and StandBy widgets, calendar auto-import and the countdown format are Premium features per its listing. 28 languages, more than any other app here.

  • Nearly 188,000 ratings at 4.81★
  • Widgets on every surface, but in Premium
  • No Dynamic Island, Live Activity or AI backgrounds in its listing
Verdict: the default recommendation for most people, provided you're fine paying for widgets.

2. Countdown Star — the budget pick with a track record

Joseph Merrill's app has 215,980 ratings at 4.77★ and has been updated for over a decade (v3.0.10 in September 2026). The free version shows ads; a one-time remove-ads purchase or a separate paid ad-free version takes them away. Apple Watch support per its listing.

  • The most ratings of any app on this list
  • Pay once, no subscription
  • No Lock Screen widgets, Dynamic Island or AI backgrounds in its listing
Verdict: the safe, cheap choice if you don't need Lock Screen or Dynamic Island.

3. Countdown: Event Countdown — Live Activity without AI

ROOT38's app is the other one here with a Dynamic Island Live Activity in its listing, alongside Lock Screen and StandBy widgets. 4.75★ from 23,872 ratings, 12 languages, and Premium is available monthly, yearly or as a one-off lifetime purchase. Free version is ad-supported.

  • Live ticking countdown in the Dynamic Island
  • Lifetime purchase available
  • Ads in the free tier; no AI backgrounds
Verdict: if you want a Live Activity and don't care about generated backgrounds, this is the direct alternative to DayDrop with a much larger rating base.

4. DayDrop — AI backgrounds that follow the countdown everywhere

Full disclosure: this is my app. It is the only one of the six whose listing includes AI-generated backgrounds, and one of two with a Dynamic Island Live Activity (with a progress ring and the exact-time countdown in hours, minutes and seconds). The generated image is attached to the event, so it appears on all 10 widget placements, the Live Activity, StandBy and the full-screen view. 16 languages. It is also by far the newest and least-rated app here: 4.6★ from 84 ratings worldwide.

  • Free: unlimited countdowns, Small and Medium Home Screen widgets, themes, photo backgrounds
  • Premium ($1.99/mo, $12.99/yr with a one-month free trial, $24.99 lifetime): AI backgrounds, Lock Screen and Large widgets, Live Activity, iCloud sync
  • Small rating base; no Apple Watch app
Verdict: choose it for the AI background and the Live Activity; choose something above if a large rating count matters more to you.

5. Days • Countdown & Widgets — clean widgets

MD Apps' Days has 4.77★ from 12,599 ratings and focuses on good-looking Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets, with 10 languages. Extras are sold as in-app purchases.

  • Well-designed widgets, Lock Screen included
  • No Dynamic Island, Live Activity or AI in its listing
Verdict: a solid mid-size option if the look of the widget is what you care about.

6. TimeCount — widget-first and free-leaning

App Forgeron's TimeCount, 4.60★ from 24,298 ratings, advertises an "unlimited free mode" and a widget-first design. Six languages. Its listing doesn't mention Lock Screen widgets, Dynamic Island or AI backgrounds.

Verdict: worth a look if you want a Home Screen widget and no paywall pressure.
