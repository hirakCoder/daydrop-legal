How to Put a Christmas Countdown on Your iPhone

Updated September 2026 · 4 min read · Real iPhone screenshots

Christmas Day 2026 falls on Friday, December 25. If you want the number of days left on your phone every time you glance at it — not buried in an app — iOS can show a Christmas countdown in four places: the Lock Screen, the Home Screen, the Dynamic Island, and StandBy mode.

This guide sets up all four with DayDrop in about two minutes. Every screenshot below is a real, unedited iPhone capture. Works on iOS 18 and iOS 26.

Step 1: Create the Christmas Countdown

STEP 1

Download DayDrop from the App Store (free, no account needed).

Tap +, name the event "Christmas", and set the date to December 25.

For the background, pick the Holiday category, use your own photo — or type a prompt like "cozy Christmas tree with warm lights" and let DayDrop's AI background generator make one for you.

Step 2: Put It on Your Lock Screen

STEP 2

Long-press your Lock Screen and tap Customize.

Tap the widget area below the clock, search for DayDrop, and add the countdown widget.

Tap Done. That's it — the days-left number now lives under your clock.

The Lock Screen widget with its progress bar — every glance at your phone shows the countdown.

Step 3: Add the Home Screen Widget

STEP 3

Long-press your Home Screen until apps jiggle, then tap +.

Search for DayDrop and choose Medium or Large.

The widget shows your Christmas photo or AI background, the days remaining, the date, and a progress bar of how much of the wait is already behind you.

A real Home Screen: DayDrop's large widget with an AI-generated tree background, sitting alongside Spotify and Calendar.

One detail people love: the "21% elapsed" progress bar. Watching the bar fill up toward December is half the fun — especially for kids counting down.

Step 4: Keep It in the Dynamic Island

On iPhone 14 Pro and later, DayDrop can run the Christmas countdown as a Live Activity in the Dynamic Island — days, hours, and minutes ticking at the top of your screen, over whatever app you're in. None of the other countdown apps we compared in our roundup list this feature.

STEP 4

Open DayDrop, tap your Christmas event, and choose Start Live Activity.

The countdown appears in the Dynamic Island and on the Lock Screen as a rich card. Long-press the Island to expand it.

Live Activities, Lock Screen and Large widgets are DayDrop Premium ($1.99/month or $24.99 lifetime). The Home Screen Small and Medium widgets are free.

Bonus: Count Down the Whole Season at Once

December isn't just Christmas. DayDrop's Large multi-event widget shows several countdowns in one place — Christmas Eve, Christmas, New Year's Eve, a winter trip — each with its own image, stacked on a single widget. Create each event, add the Large widget, and it rotates the season for you.

And when you put your iPhone on a charger sideways, StandBy mode turns it into a bedside Christmas countdown clock — genuinely great in a kid's room throughout December.
