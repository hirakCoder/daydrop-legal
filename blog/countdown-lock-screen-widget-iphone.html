Countdown Widget on the iPhone Lock Screen: Setup and 8 Apps Compared

Updated October 7, 2026 · 6 min read · iOS 18 and iOS 26

Short answer: iOS has no built-in countdown widget, so install a countdown app that supports Lock Screen widgets, create the event, then press and hold the Lock Screen, tap Customize → Lock Screen, tap the strip under the clock and add the app's circular, rectangular or inline widget. It takes about two minutes.

One limit to know before you start: Lock Screen widgets refresh on Apple's schedule, so they show days (and sometimes hours), not ticking seconds. If you want seconds, use a Live Activity instead; the section below explains the difference.

The three Lock Screen widget shapes

Every Lock Screen widget on iPhone is one of three shapes, and the shape decides what a countdown can show.

ShapeWhere it sitsBest forWhat a countdown shows
CircularStrip under the clock (1 of 4 slots)One number at a glanceDays left, often with a progress ring
RectangularStrip under the clock (2 of 4 slots)Name plus numberEvent name, days left, sometimes the date
InlineThe single line above the clockText only, always visible"42 days to Lisbon" style text

The strip under the clock has four small slots. You can fill it with four circular widgets, two rectangular ones, or one rectangular and two circular. Add the inline line above the clock and you can keep five countdowns on one Lock Screen.

How to add the widget (iOS 18 and iOS 26)

STEP 1

Install a countdown app that offers Lock Screen widgets and create your event in the app. In DayDrop, tap +, enter the name and date, and save.

STEP 2

Wake the phone and unlock with Face ID, but stay on the Lock Screen (don't swipe up). Press and hold an empty area until the screen zooms out and Customize appears.

STEP 3

Tap Customize, then Lock Screen on the left. The widget strip under the clock becomes editable.

STEP 4

Tap the strip, find the app in the list, and choose circular or rectangular. For an inline widget, tap the date line above the clock instead and pick the app there.

STEP 5

Tap the widget you just placed to choose which countdown it shows (most apps default to the next event). Tap Done, then Done again.

If the app doesn't appear in the widget list, open it once first; iOS registers an app's widgets on first launch. If a widget shows placeholder text, tap it and pick an event.

Why a Lock Screen widget can't tick every second

Widgets on iOS are snapshots. The system asks the app for a timeline of images and shows them on a schedule it controls, with a daily budget of refreshes to protect battery. A countdown that changes once a day fits that model perfectly; a countdown that changes every second does not, and Apple doesn't allow widgets to redraw that often. That is why every app in the table below shows days, and at best hours, on the Lock Screen.

The exception is a Live Activity. Live Activities are the Lock Screen and Dynamic Island elements that sports scores and food-delivery apps use, and they can run a true ticking timer. DayDrop's Live Activity shows days, hours, minutes and seconds with a progress ring, in the Dynamic Island and on the Lock Screen, for events with a set time. If seconds matter to you, that is the feature to look for; see the Dynamic Island countdown guide.

Frequently asked questions

Does iOS have a built-in countdown widget for the Lock Screen?

No. iOS 18 and iOS 26 ship Lock Screen widgets for Calendar, Reminders, Weather, Clock, Fitness and Batteries, but none of them counts down to a date you choose. You need a third-party countdown app.

Why doesn't my Lock Screen countdown widget show seconds?

Apple limits how often widgets can redraw to protect battery life, so a Lock Screen widget refreshes on a schedule rather than every second. For a live ticking countdown use a Live Activity, which runs on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island.

How many countdown widgets fit on the Lock Screen?

Up to four small slots under the clock (four circular, two rectangular, or one rectangular plus two circular) and one inline widget above the clock, so five countdowns at once.

Are Lock Screen countdown widgets free?

Every app above is free to download. Most, including DayDrop, put Lock Screen widgets in a paid tier or behind an in-app purchase. DayDrop's Home Screen widgets are free; Lock Screen widgets are part of Premium, which has a one-month free trial on the yearly plan.

Can I have a different countdown on each Lock Screen?

Yes. Each Lock Screen keeps its own widgets, so pair a holiday countdown with one wallpaper and a trip countdown with another, then swipe between them.

My widget stopped updating. What do I do?

Open the app once (widgets are refreshed when the app runs), check that Background App Refresh is on for it, and make sure Low Power Mode is off. The full checklist is in Countdown widget not updating on iPhone: 7 fixes.
