Planner app or countdown app?

Two different kinds of app answer "wedding countdown," and the right one depends on what you want to see.

  • Planner apps (The Knot, Zola, WeddingWire) manage the guest list, budget, vendors and website, and show a days-to-go number inside the app. Zola's listing mentions a widget; The Knot's and WeddingWire's don't. If you already live in one of these, check whether its countdown is enough before adding anything.
  • Countdown apps do one thing: put the number where you'll see it without opening anything, with your own photo behind it. Lock Screen widgets, StandBy on the nightstand, and in two apps a Live Activity that ticks in the Dynamic Island on the day.

Many couples run both: the planner for the work, a countdown widget for the feeling.

Set it up: wedding countdown on Home Screen, Lock Screen and Dynamic Island

STEP 1

Create the event with the ceremony date and time. The time matters: it lets the countdown tick to the exact moment on the day, and a Live Activity needs it.

STEP 2

Pick the background. Your engagement photo from the library works in every countdown app here. In DayDrop you can also describe it: the prompts below produce backgrounds that leave room for the number.

STEP 3

Home Screen: long-press the Home Screen, tap Edit → Add Widget, choose the app and a size. Medium shows the name, number and photo; Small is just the number.

STEP 4

Lock Screen: press and hold the Lock Screen, Customize → Lock Screen, tap the strip under the clock, pick the app's circular (number in a ring) or rectangular (name + number) widget. Full detail in the Lock Screen guide.

STEP 5

Dynamic Island, wedding week: open the event and tap Go Live (DayDrop) or the Live Activity button. Days with a progress ring at first, then hours, minutes and seconds in the final day. Explained in the live countdown guide.

AI background prompts that work for a wedding widget

vineyard at golden hour, soft haze, empty sky above the rows, no people
white peonies and eucalyptus on linen, overhead, soft natural light, lots of negative space
beach ceremony arch at sunset, pastel sky, wide open space in the upper half

Keep the subject low or to one side so the number sits on open sky or fabric. Avoid prompts with text or faces; the model's lettering fights the countdown and faces land under the number.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best wedding countdown app for iPhone?

If you already use a planner, The Knot and Zola include a countdown and Zola has a widget. Can I put a wedding countdown on my iPhone Lock Screen?

Yes, with any app that lists Lock Screen widgets: Countdown by Find Appiness, Countdown: Event Countdown, Days, Countdown Widget Maker or DayDrop. Press and hold the Lock Screen, Customize, Lock Screen, tap the strip under the clock and add the widget.

Can the countdown use our engagement photo?

Yes in every dedicated countdown app here. In DayDrop the photo is attached to the event and shows on Home Screen widgets, Lock Screen widgets, the Dynamic Island Live Activity and StandBy.

Can it show hours, minutes and seconds?

Set the ceremony time and use a Live Activity (Countdown: Event Countdown or DayDrop). DayDrop's widgets also tick in the final 24 hours.

Can both of us share the same countdown?

iCloud sync keeps your own devices identical; between two people, send a share card or each create the same event. Planner apps share the whole workspace between partners.
