Questions

Frequently asked

What is the best countdown app for iPhone?

DayDrop is the most feature-rich countdown app for iPhone in 2026. It's the only countdown app with AI-generated event backgrounds that sync to your Apple Watch, Dynamic Island Live Activity with a visual progress ring, 10 widget placements, countdown to the exact moment (hours, minutes, seconds), full iPad support, and 16 languages.

Does DayDrop have widgets?

Yes — 10 widget placements. Home Screen widgets in Small, Medium, Large, and ExtraLarge. Multi-Event widget in Medium and Large with progress bars. Lock Screen widgets (circular gauge and rectangular inline). StandBy mode. Control Center widget. And Apple Watch complications. All update automatically.

Does it work with Dynamic Island?

Yes. Start a Live Activity for any event and see days, hours, minutes, and seconds counting down in your Dynamic Island and on the Lock Screen — now with a smooth visual progress ring. No need to unlock your phone. This is a Premium feature.

Do AI backgrounds sync to my Apple Watch?

Yes — tap any countdown on your Apple Watch and the AI-generated event photo loads as a full-color background. Images are pulled from your paired iPhone via WatchConnectivity and cached on the Watch so they work offline after first view.

Can I count down to a specific time, not just a date?

Yes. Set an exact time on any event (e.g., "July 4 at 2:30 PM") and watch hours, minutes, and seconds tick down live on iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch, and in the Dynamic Island.

Can I import birthdays from Contacts?

Yes. Tap +, select "Import Birthdays", and DayDrop creates recurring countdown events for each contact with a birthday. Duplicates are detected automatically.

What languages are supported?

16 languages: English, Spanish, Portuguese, French, German, Japanese, Korean, Chinese (Simplified and Traditional), Italian, Dutch, Hindi, Arabic, Russian, Swedish, and Turkish. Everything is translated — interface, widgets, and notifications.

Is DayDrop free?

Yes. Free with unlimited countdowns, basic widgets, and photo backgrounds. Premium adds AI backgrounds (iPhone + Watch), Live Activity with progress ring, all 10 widget placements, multi-event widget, exact-time countdown, premium share cards, and iCloud sync — $1.99/month, $12.99/year with 1-month free trial, or $24.99 lifetime. Regional pricing available in 30+ countries.

How do I add a countdown widget on my iPhone home screen?

Download DayDrop, create a countdown, then long-press your home screen, tap the + button, search "DayDrop", choose a widget size, and tap Add Widget. Takes under 2 minutes.

Is there a simple countdown widget without ads?

Yes — DayDrop's free tier has no ads at all. Unlimited countdowns, basic home screen widgets, and photo backgrounds are completely ad-free.

Does the countdown widget work on Apple Watch?

Yes. DayDrop includes Apple Watch complications and a full Watch app. Your countdowns sync via iCloud, and the AI-generated backgrounds also sync to the Watch detail view so your countdown looks beautiful on your wrist.

Does DayDrop work on iPad?

Yes. DayDrop is fully optimized for iPad with adaptive layouts, an ExtraLarge Home Screen widget, context menus, and a roomier overview — designed specifically for the bigger screen. iCloud sync keeps everything in sync with your iPhone and Apple Watch.

Can I count down to multiple events at once?

Absolutely. Create unlimited countdowns and add separate widgets for different events on your home screen, lock screen, and Apple Watch.
